How it works

1

Get your passcode

Ask your team admin for a personal passcode. Each person gets their own — access is private and admin-controlled.

2

Enter & pick a game

Tap "Enter with passcode", type your code, and you're in the lobby. Pick a game and create or join a room.

3

Share the room code

Share the 3-word room code with your team. Once everyone joins, the host clicks Start and you're playing.
